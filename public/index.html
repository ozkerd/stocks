TimesFM-3 Foundation Model Technology & Mathematical Accuracy

TimesFM (Time Series Foundation Model) is Google Research's state-of-the-art pretrained decoder-only foundation model engineered specifically for time-series forecasting. Unlike traditional autoregressive models or simple recurrent networks, TimesFM-3 tokenizes continuous sequences into semantic patches and incorporates multi-horizon temporal self-attention.

This implementation injects multivariate exogenous covariates—including Treasury yield curves, the CBOE VIX risk barometer, commodities, and stochastic momentum indicators—directly into the predictive manifold. The expanding confidence cone mathematically captures quantile uncertainty (\(P_{10}\) to \(P_{90}\)), delivering calibrated probabilistic bounds with rigorous statistical fidelity.
